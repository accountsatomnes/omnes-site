Our vision.

Compute now underpins the modern economy. Omnes is building its financial layer, so that the yield of productive compute can be held as an institutional-grade, tokenized security.

The market

Artificial intelligence, Bitcoin, digital finance and the infrastructure of global technology all run on compute. Yet the capital behind it moves through a fragmented industry, with little transparency and few routes in for investors who need an institutional structure before they allocate.

Our approach

Omnes aggregates yield-generating compute assets across that industry, structures them into secured debt instruments through a Luxembourg securitisation fund, and issues them as onchain securities for professional investors in eligible jurisdictions.

We start with Bitcoin mining. The Omnes Mining Note gives professional investors exposure to Bitcoin mining economics without owning, operating or maintaining mining hardware.

How we build.

Three principles shape every Omnes product, from legal structure to onchain settlement.

  • Luxembourg structure

    Luxembourg-structured, a structure widely used for institutional products. Our securitisation fund provides segregated compartments for each series, in a structure institutional investors know.

  • Tier 1 partners

    Partners in the Omnes structure are chosen for their regulatory standing, operational track record and institutional credibility, from legal counsel and corporate services to custody and tokenization.

  • Institutional structure, tokenized form

    Omnes products are structured to meet the compliance and reporting requirements of traditional institutional investors, with ownership recorded in the issuer’s register and mirrored onchain. We bridge institutional capital markets and blockchain infrastructure without compromising on either side.

Our partners.

The notes are issued by Omnes Securities Fund, a Luxembourg unregulated securitisation fund, not supervised by the CSSF, and managed by Omnes Securities S.à r.l. Hashrate is supplied by Omnes Technology AG, the Hashrate Aggregator and an Omnes affiliate. Every other role in the structure is held by an independent institution.

Corporate service provider
Apex Fund Services S.A.
Corporate services for the issuer in Luxembourg.
Custodian
Zodia Custody (Europe) S.A.
A crypto-asset service provider authorised by the CSSF under MiCA, holding the notes’ Bitcoin in a dedicated custody account, in a custodian capacity only.
Security agent
Apex Corporate Trustees (UK) Limited
Holds, administers and enforces the pledges granted in favour of noteholders.
Tokenization
Tokeny S.à r.l.
The tokenization platform for the note, an ERC-3643 token on Base.
Auditor

Auditor of the issuer’s financial statements, PwC Luxembourg.
