01Overview

This cookies policy outlines the cookies that are collected on the website: www.omnes.io.

02Why do we use cookies?

When we conduct business with our clients, we primarily do so over the internet by way of devices such as computers and mobile devices. In order to make our websites work properly and to help us understand the activities of our clients and other users, we sometimes place small data files called cookies. In this cookie notice, we outline the types of cookies we use.

03What are cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves on your browser when you visit the site.

04We only collect necessary cookies

Necessary Cookies are enabled by default and are necessary to enable you to move around our websites and take advantage of their features. Without these cookies, essential security features cannot be activated and services you have asked for cannot be provided.

05You cannot opt out of necessary cookies

You have the right to accept or reject certain types of optional cookies. We do not collect optional cookies. We only collect necessary cookies that you cannot opt out of or reject.

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. For further details, please visit the following website: www.aboutcookies.org (opens in a new tab).

You can delete all cookies that are already on your computer and you can set most browsers to prevent them from being placed. If you do this, please note that you may have to manually adjust some preferences each time you visit our website or use our platform. Furthermore, a number of services and functionalities we offer may not work.

We take cyber security matters seriously and we are committed to keeping your personal information safe. For more information on how we handle your data, see the Global Privacy Notice on our website.

06How to contact us

If you have questions or comments about our cookies practices, you can contact us via one of the methods set out below: legal@omnes.io.

07Changes to our cookies policy

Any changes we make to our cookies policy in the future will be posted on this page and, where appropriate, notified to you by e-mail. Please check back frequently to see any updates or changes to our cookies policy.
